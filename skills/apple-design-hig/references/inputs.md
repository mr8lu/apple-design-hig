# Inputs

- **Category**: Apple Human Interface Guidelines
- **Slug**: `inputs`
- **Official URL**: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/inputs

**Summary**: Learn about the various methods people use to control your app or game and enter data.

- **Action button** (`action-button`): The Action button gives people quick access to their favorite features on supported iPhone and Apple Watch models.
- **Apple Pencil and Scribble** (`apple-pencil-and-scribble`): Apple Pencil helps make drawing, handwriting, and marking effortless and natural, in addition to performing well as a pointer and UI interaction tool.
- **Camera Control** (`camera-control`): The Camera Control provides direct access to your app’s camera experience.
- **Digital Crown** (`digital-crown`): The Digital Crown is an important hardware input for Apple Vision Pro and Apple Watch.
- **Eyes** (`eyes`): In visionOS, people look at a virtual object to identify it as a target they can interact with.
- **Focus and selection** (`focus-and-selection`): Focus helps people visually confirm the object that their interaction targets.
- **Game controls** (`game-controls`): Precise, intuitive game controls enhance gameplay and can increase a player’s immersion in the game.
- **Gestures** (`gestures`): A gesture is a physical motion that a person uses to directly affect an object in an app or game on their device.
- **Gyroscope and accelerometer** (`gyro-and-accelerometer`): On-device gyroscopes and accelerometers can supply data about a device’s movement in the physical world.
- **Keyboards** (`keyboards`): A physical keyboard can be an essential input device for entering text, playing games, controlling apps, and more.
- **Nearby interactions** (`nearby-interactions`): Nearby interactions support on-device experiences that integrate the presence of people and objects in the nearby environment.
- **Pointing devices** (`pointing-devices`): People can use a pointing device like a trackpad or mouse to navigate the interface and initiate actions.
- **Remotes** (`remotes`): The Siri Remote is the primary input method for Apple TV, helping people feel connected to onscreen content from across the room.
